Privacy policy

Task Forge Relay is a personal-use utility operated by Stephen Jones. It connects a configured Google Sheets mailbox to his local TaskForge scheduler.

Google data accessed and used

The relay requests the Google Sheets permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets. That permission allows broad spreadsheet access. The relay’s configuration directs it to one spreadsheet mailbox, where it reads task instructions, task identifiers, delivery status, and related metadata.

It uses those records to submit validated tasks to the local scheduler and to update the mailbox with delivery receipts, errors, and selected task reports. Tasks may invoke separately configured local workers or external services; what those workers do depends on the operator’s task configuration and authorization.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in private files on the operator’s computer, outside the application repository. Local task records, reports, delivery receipts, and operational logs are stored on that computer. TaskForge backups may also retain task records and reports. Mailbox records and returned reports remain in Google Sheets.

There is no automatic deletion schedule for these records. The operator controls deletion of local records, backups, and spreadsheet content. Revoking Google access stops future authorized access but does not delete records already stored.

Sharing

The relay sends mailbox updates and selected reports to Google Sheets. People or services with access to the configured spreadsheet can see that content according to its sharing permissions. The local scheduler and its configured workers receive task instructions needed to carry out their assigned work.

The relay itself has no advertising, data-sale, or general-purpose AI model training functionality. Google data is used for the described task transport and result-return functions. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This information website

These static pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no sign-in form, analytics script, advertising code, or relay data. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy statement.
